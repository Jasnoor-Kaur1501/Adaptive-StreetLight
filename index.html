Control logic

  1. LDR reads ambient light. Daylight: all lamps OFF.
  2. Night, nothing nearby: lamp holds at dim level.
  3. Motion sensor detects a vehicle or person within range: lamp goes to 100%.
  4. After the object leaves, the lamp holds for a short delay, then fades back to dim.
